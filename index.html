Career Summary

I keep stock records accurate, so businesses can trust their numbers and act on them.

I am an inventory professional with experience across hypermarkets, retail chains, warehouses and auto parts, in Kuwait, Saudi Arabia and India. I started in 2018 as a spare parts coordinator and have since worked as an inventory coordinator, controller and senior controller. Today I work as an Inventory Auditor at City Hypermarket in Kuwait.

My day-to-day is counting, checking and explaining the gaps. I set up cycle counting, reconcile stock, investigate purchase and sales discrepancies, and report audit findings to senior management. I also forecast demand from sales history and trends, and watch aging stock so teams can act early. I train people too, with 50+ employees taught inventory management and loss prevention.

My biggest project was leading the ERP migration at Grand Hypermarket in 2023, delivered on time across 32 hypermarkets. I am now looking for an Inventory Manager or Senior Inventory Controller role, where I can help a business improve its operations and grow.
